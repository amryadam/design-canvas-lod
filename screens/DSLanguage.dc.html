Rules

  • Layout is logical: ms- me- ps- pe- start end, never left or right. The side menu sits at the start, the account chip at the end, in both languages.
  • The document flips with dir; the Arabic face applies to everything under it, so ng-zorro components switch with the page.
  • Codes, emails, addresses, money and counts are LTR islands: dir="ltr" and the mono face, aligned to the start of their column.
  • Digits are Latin in both languages. Money is written SAR 412,800; dates show the Gregorian day and the Hijri day beside it where a deadline matters.
  • Icons that carry direction mirror: chevrons, the login and logout arrows, the send arrow, the fold and unfold, the question mark. Checks, clocks, stars and pictograms do not.
  • Copy is written for both languages at once: one sentence, no idiom, no exclamation mark. Errors say what happened and what to do next.
  • Text fields hold either script; the field itself does not switch direction on its own, the page does.
