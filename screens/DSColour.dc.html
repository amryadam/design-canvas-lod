Design system · 01

Colour

Ink on paper. Black is the only accent; every surface is a warm neutral, never a cool grey; the state colours are the only saturated values and are reserved for state.

Rules

  • A colour is a token. Components say bg-success-tint text-success, never a hex, so dark mode comes for free.
  • State colours are for state. A role chip, a filter or a count never borrows success, warning or error.
  • Text stays ink inside a banner or a tinted row; only the icon and the ground carry the tone.
  • Ink marks the row you are on. The star is the one thing that is yellow, so a favorite reads at a glance.
  • In dark mode the accent inverts to off-white: a filled button is still the strongest thing on the page.
  • Measured: ink on paper 16:1, muted 6.2:1, faint 4.2:1 on paper and 4.5:1 on a surface. Faint is for labels and controls, never body copy. Each state colour clears 4.5:1 on white, on paper and on its own tint, and its dark-mode step clears the same on the night ground. A chip still carries an icon and a word; colour is never the only signal.
