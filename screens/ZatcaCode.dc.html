Where to get it
  1. Sign in to fatoora.zatca.gov.sa with the company's ZATCA account.
  2. Choose Onboard new solution unit, then Generate OTP for 1 device.
  3. Type the code here within the hour and press Send.
What this sets upSeaturtle becomes a registered e-invoicing solution for Marasy on the ZATCA (Fatoora) platform.
