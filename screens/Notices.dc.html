Rules
  • The title names what failed or what happened, never “Something went wrong”. The body says what it means and what to do.
  • A 3px edge in the tone colour and a tinted icon tile carry the state. Text stays ink.
  • The reference is a mono chip with an explicit Copy, not a hidden click target. Copy turns into a tick for 1.5 s.
  • One optional action, outlined, never two. Errors and sign-in notices stay; success, warning and info run down a 2px timer, paused on hover.
