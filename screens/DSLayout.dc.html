Screen sizes — the rule

  • Every screen is designed at the standard size set, the same one sign-up uses: 1920×1080 desktop, 2560×1440 (2K), 1440×900 laptop, 834 tablet and 390 phone.
  • The size set is the main design of a screen. Its states — errors, menus open, the rail, nested pages, RTL — are drawn once, at 1440×900, and follow the mains.
  • Every artboard title states its size, at the end, as W×H. A frame without a size in its title is not finished.
  • The shell does not scale with the screen: the 248 menu, 60 bar and 48 breadcrumb card stay at 1× and the content panel takes the room. Below md the menu is the drawer; from md the desktop shell, folded to the rail on a tablet.
