Space, shape and depth

A 4px grid, three radii and one pill, three control heights, two shadows. Density comes from the grid, not from smaller type.

Inside a control 12 to 14, between fields 16, between sections 24 to 32, page padding 16 on a phone, 24 on a tablet, 32 on a laptop and up. A card pads 20 to 24. Nothing is ever 6, 10 or 14 tall or wide, but a control's inner padding may be.

Radii

sm 6px What sits inside a rounded container: a dropdown item, a checkbox, a chip, the raised language segment.
md 8px Buttons, inputs, selects, the workspace card.
lg 12px Cards, dialogs, drawers, the notice.
pill 9999px Counts, avatars, filter chips.

Anything a finger presses is 44px tall, including the 32px row action's hit box. A menu row is 40; a rail square is 44; an icon button is 36 to 44 by its place.

Depth

Surface 0 1px 2px 4 % Resting cards sit almost flat. The hairline does the work; the shadow only lifts it off the page.
Overlay 0 24px 48px −8px 20 % Dialogs, drawers, dropdowns, the notice. The only thing that floats.
Lines line · line-strong Dividers and card edges take the hairline. Anything you can type into or press takes the strong line.
