Typography

Three faces, one job each. Sizes are drawn from the screens, not from a modular scale: the ramp below is every size the product uses.

Faces

Latin · UI
Inter
Variable, 400 to 700. Self-hosted, subset to Latin and Latin Extended. Body 14px, never below 11px.
Arabic · UI
آي بي إم بلكس
IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400, 500, 600, 700. Applied to the whole document under [dir='rtl']; Latin inside an Arabic page falls through to Inter by unicode-range.
Mono · data
JetBrains Mono
Variable, 400 to 600. Codes, money, usernames, emails, addresses and counts. Always dir="ltr", tabular numerals.

Rules

  • One weight step marks emphasis: 600 against 400. Bold 700 is for the page title and the KPI value only.
  • Numbers people compare sit in the mono face with tabular numerals, right-aligned in a table: money, duties, counts.
  • Codes and addresses never wrap and never translate: CL-2026-0412, marasy.seaturtle.app, tariq@marasy.sa.
  • Labels in capitals are 11px, 600, letter-spaced 0.06em and faint. They name a group, never a value.
  • Arabic text is never letter-spaced, never faux-bold and never set in Inter.
  • Line length stays under 76 characters; a description under a field is one sentence.
