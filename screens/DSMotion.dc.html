Design system · 05

Motion and states

Three durations, one easing for changes in place and a pair for the drawer. Every control has the same five states, drawn the same way.

Durations

120ms
Fast A colour, a border, a focus ring, the star fading in.
200ms
Base A panel opening: dropdown, popover, the tooltip, a branch expanding.
300ms
Slow A page-level move: the drawer sliding in, the rail folding, the notice arriving.

Easing

In and out cubic-bezier(0.645, 0.045, 0.355, 1) The default for anything that changes in place.
Drawer in cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1) Decelerates: the drawer lands softly.
Drawer out cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.9, 0.6) Accelerates: it leaves like a swipe.
Reduced motion none Every transition drops to an instant change; nothing is ever only communicated by motion.
