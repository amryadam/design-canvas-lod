Chip styles

Seven ways to draw the same six chips: four states, two roles. Each row is shown on a white card and on the night surface. E, the soft tag, is the chosen style for state and for kinds; the add-ons follow it.

ATinted pill

The previous chip: tone at 12% on the ground, tone text, pill.

Reads well on white and paper. In a dense table a row of six tints gets loud.
