Voice

  • One sentence per message. A banner, a notice, a description under a field: never two.
  • Name the thing: "Disable Huda Al-Qahtani?", not "Are you sure?". "Held by customs", not "Error".
  • An error says what happened and what to do next. It never blames and never exclaims.
  • Buttons are verbs: Continue, Sign in, Disable user, Review invoices. Never OK, Submit or Yes.
  • Numbers people act on are shown, not described: "2 past their deadline", not "some overdue".
